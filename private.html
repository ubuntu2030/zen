Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

ZenAmbient ("we", "our", or "us") is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first approach. We believe your personal relaxation space should remain entirely private.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage telemetry, location data, or unique device identifiers. ZenAmbient operates without remote analytics or advertising tracking systems.

2. Local Storage Only

All your user preferences, custom soundscape mixes, timer configurations, and application state are stored strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the application.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

Transactions for ZenAmbient Pro (Subscriptions or Lifetime Access) are processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We do not host payment servers or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple/Google account credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

ZenAmbient does not integrate third-party SDKs, ad networks, or external analytics frameworks (e.g., no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no advertising IDs).

5. Contact Us
