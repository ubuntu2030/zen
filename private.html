Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZenAmbient ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application ZenAmbient (package name com.tsw.zenambient on Google Play). It applies to the Android app and our hosted policy pages. By using the app, you agree to this policy. See also our Terms of Service.

1. Summary

ZenAmbient is built around offline playback of built-in ambient sounds. We do not operate our own servers to collect personal profiles, accounts, or usage analytics. Most information stays on your device. When you buy ZenAmbient Pro, Google Play processes payment and entitlement; we receive only what Google’s billing APIs provide to verify your purchase.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store the following locally (for example via on-device storage libraries):

  • Settings (background playback, fade-out, restore last mix, and similar preferences)
  • Custom mixes, presets, and timer preferences
  • Whether you have accepted this policy and whether Pro features are unlocked

This data is not transmitted to us. It is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not, on our own systems:

  • Require you to create an account with us
  • Collect your name, email, phone number, or postal address through the app
  • Run our own advertising or behavioral analytics (no ad SDK in the Android app)
  • Sell your personal information

4. Google Play & Network Use

Although sound playback works offline, the app may use the network for:

  • Google Play Billing — subscriptions and one-time purchases (e.g. annual subscription, lifetime unlock), including restore purchases
  • Google Play In-App Review — optional store rating flow when you choose "Rate on Google Play"
  • Opening this Privacy Policy and Terms in an in-app browser when you tap the links

Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. Purchase status is verified through Google’s services. Google’s use of data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and your Google Play account settings.

Components such as Google Play Billing libraries are provided by Google; they may process device and purchase-related data as described in Google’s documentation. We do not embed third-party advertising networks or our own analytics SDKs in the Android app.

5. Permissions

Depending on your device and Android version, the app may request capabilities reflected in the store listing, such as:

  • Internet / network state — billing, policy pages, and connectivity checks used by Play services
  • Foreground service (media playback) — continue playback when the screen is off, with a media-style notification where required by the system

Permissions are used only for the features described above and in the app’s on-screen disclosures.

6. Children

ZenAmbient is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you delete local app data by uninstalling or clearing storage.

7. Security & Your Choices

Because data stays on your device, you control it through your device settings: you may clear app storage, revoke permissions, or uninstall the app. For subscription management, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

8. International Users

If you use ZenAmbient outside your home country, Google Play may process purchase data according to its own policies. Our app does not transfer your mix or preference data to our servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes may also be reflected in the app before or at the time they take effect. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
